01 - BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
========================

PURPOSE: Analyze participant responses (accuracy, speed, confidence) to answer
whether people can reliably perceive Big Five traits from short face videos.

DATA NEEDED:
- participant_*.csv files (columns: trait, response_correct, response_time, confidence_rating)

ANALYSES TO RUN:
----------------

1. OVERALL ACCURACY vs CHANCE (50%)
   - Calculate mean accuracy across all participants and all trials
   - One-sample t-test: is overall accuracy significantly above 50%?
   - Report: mean accuracy, SD, t-statistic, p-value, Cohen's d

2. ACCURACY PER TRAIT
   - Calculate mean accuracy for each of the 5 Big Five traits
   - One-sample t-tests for each trait against 50% chance
   - Apply Bonferroni or FDR correction for multiple comparisons (5 tests)
   - Rank traits by perceptibility
   - Visualization: bar chart with error bars and significance stars

3. RESPONSE TIME ANALYSIS
   - Mean RT for correct vs incorrect trials (paired t-test)
   - Mean RT per trait (one-way repeated-measures ANOVA)
   - RT distribution: check for outliers (>3 SD from mean, or <200ms)
   - Visualization: violin plots of RT by trait, split by correct/incorrect

4. SPEED-ACCURACY TRADEOFF
   - Correlation between RT and accuracy per participant
   - Are faster responses more or less accurate?
   - Scatter plot: mean RT vs mean accuracy per participant

5. PRACTICE/FATIGUE EFFECTS
   - Split trials into early (first 40) vs late (last 85) blocks
   - Compare accuracy and RT between blocks
   - Check if performance improves (learning) or declines (fatigue)

STATISTICAL METHODS:
- One-sample t-test (accuracy vs 50%)
- Paired t-test (correct vs incorrect RT)
- Repeated-measures ANOVA (trait differences)
- Pearson correlation (speed-accuracy)
- Effect sizes: Cohen's d for t-tests, eta-squared for ANOVA

OUTPUT FILES TO GENERATE:
- behavioral_summary_stats.csv
- accuracy_by_trait.png
- rt_by_trait.png
- rt_correct_vs_incorrect.png
