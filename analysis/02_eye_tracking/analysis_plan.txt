02 - EYE TRACKING ANALYSIS
==========================

PURPOSE: Analyze gaze behavior during personality perception trials.
Understand WHERE people look and HOW they look when judging personality.

DATA NEEDED:
- EDF files from EyeLink (converted to ASC or parsed with pylink)
- Fixation reports, saccade reports, sample reports
- Interest area definitions (video regions on screen)

PREREQUISITE - EDF DATA EXTRACTION:
- Use SR Research Data Viewer to export fixation/saccade reports
- OR use pyedfread / edf2asc tool to convert EDF -> ASCII
- Key columns needed: timestamp, x_position, y_position, pupil_size,
  fixation_duration, saccade_amplitude, event_type

ANALYSES TO RUN:
----------------

1. FIXATION METRICS (per trial)
   - Total fixation count during each video
   - Mean fixation duration (ms)
   - Total dwell time on each video
   - First fixation location and duration
   - Last fixation before response
   - Compare: fixations on chosen vs unchosen video

2. SACCADE METRICS
   - Saccade count per trial
   - Mean saccade amplitude (degrees)
   - Saccade velocity profiles
   - Number of regressive saccades (back to previously viewed regions)

3. PUPIL SIZE ANALYSIS
   - Mean pupil size during video viewing
   - Pupil dilation changes: baseline vs during video
   - Pupil size difference between correct and incorrect trials
   - Pupil size as indicator of cognitive effort/difficulty
   - NOTE: Pupil data needs baseline correction (subtract pre-trial fixation)

4. AREA OF INTEREST (AOI) ANALYSIS
   - Define AOIs: eyes, nose, mouth, forehead, chin regions of the face
   - Dwell time per AOI for each trait
   - Do people look at different face regions for different traits?
   - Example hypothesis: mouth region more important for Extraversion/Agreeableness
   - NOTE: AOIs need to be defined on the video frames (face landmark detection)

5. GAZE-CHOICE RELATIONSHIP
   - Total looking time on Video 1 vs Video 2
   - "Gaze cascade effect": do people look more at the video they eventually choose?
   - Last fixated video before response - does it predict choice?

6. SCANPATH ANALYSIS
   - Sequence of fixations during video viewing
   - Scanpath similarity between participants (using ScanMatch or MultiMatch)
   - Do people follow similar viewing patterns for same videos?

STATISTICAL METHODS:
- Paired t-tests (chosen vs unchosen video metrics)
- Mixed-effects models (participant as random effect, trait as fixed effect)
- Correlation: gaze metrics vs accuracy/confidence
- Linear mixed models using statsmodels or pingouin

OUTPUT FILES TO GENERATE:
- fixation_summary_by_trait.csv
- gaze_heatmaps/ (per trait, per video)
- pupil_timecourse.png
- aoi_dwell_time_by_trait.png
- scanpath_examples.png
