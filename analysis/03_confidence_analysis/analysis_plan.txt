03 - CONFIDENCE ANALYSIS
========================

PURPOSE: Investigate the relationship between participants' confidence in their
personality judgments and their actual accuracy. This tests metacognitive awareness.

DATA NEEDED:
- participant_*.csv (columns: confidence_rating, response_correct, trait, response_time)

ANALYSES TO RUN:
----------------

1. CONFIDENCE DISTRIBUTION
   - Overall distribution of confidence ratings (1-5)
   - Mean confidence per trait
   - Are some traits judged with more confidence than others?
   - Visualization: histogram of confidence ratings, grouped bar by trait

2. CONFIDENCE-ACCURACY CORRELATION
   - Point-biserial correlation: confidence (1-5) vs accuracy (0/1) per trial
   - Gamma correlation (ordinal): better for ordinal confidence scales
   - Per-participant: each person's confidence-accuracy relationship
   - Are confident judgments actually more accurate? (calibration)

3. CONFIDENCE CALIBRATION CURVE
   - Group trials by confidence level (1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
   - Calculate actual accuracy at each confidence level
   - Perfect calibration = diagonal line
   - Overconfidence = accuracy below confidence level
   - Visualization: calibration plot (confidence vs actual accuracy)

4. CONFIDENCE BY TRAIT
   - Mean confidence for correct vs incorrect trials, per trait
   - 2-way ANOVA: Trait x Correctness on confidence
   - Which traits produce highest/lowest confidence?
   - Does confidence predict accuracy equally well for all traits?

5. CONFIDENCE AND RESPONSE TIME
   - Correlation between confidence and RT
   - Are faster responses associated with higher confidence?
   - Do high-confidence correct trials have different RT than high-confidence incorrect?

6. SIGNAL DETECTION ANALYSIS (ADVANCED)
   - Treat confidence as a continuous measure of decision strength
   - Type 2 ROC analysis: how well does confidence discriminate correct from incorrect
   - Meta-d' (metacognitive sensitivity): formal measure of metacognitive ability
   - Compare meta-d' across traits

STATISTICAL METHODS:
- Point-biserial / Gamma correlation
- Repeated-measures ANOVA (trait x correctness on confidence)
- Logistic regression: confidence predicting accuracy
- Signal detection theory (Type 2 SDT)

OUTPUT FILES TO GENERATE:
- confidence_distribution.png
- calibration_curve.png
- confidence_by_trait_and_accuracy.png
- confidence_rt_scatter.png
- metacognitive_sensitivity.csv
