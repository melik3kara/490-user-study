04 - TRAIT COMPARISON ANALYSIS
===============================

PURPOSE: Compare perception across the 5 Big Five traits to determine which
personality dimensions are most/least perceptible from face videos.

DATA NEEDED:
- Behavioral data (accuracy, RT, confidence per trait)
- Eye tracking data (fixation metrics per trait)

ANALYSES TO RUN:
----------------

1. TRAIT PERCEPTIBILITY RANKING
   - Rank all 5 traits by mean accuracy: Extraversion, Agreeableness,
     Conscientiousness, Emotional Stability, Openness
   - Repeated-measures ANOVA: does accuracy differ across traits?
   - Post-hoc pairwise comparisons (Tukey HSD or Bonferroni)
   - Expected: Extraversion likely most perceptible (literature suggests this)

2. TRAIT DIFFICULTY PROFILE
   - For each trait, create a "difficulty profile":
     * Accuracy (higher = easier)
     * Response time (lower = easier)
     * Confidence (higher = easier)
     * Fixation count (lower = easier, less visual search needed)
   - Radar/spider chart showing all metrics per trait

3. TRAIT-SPECIFIC GAZE PATTERNS
   - Do people look at different face regions for different traits?
   - Hypothesis: Extraversion judgments focus on mouth (smile)
   - Hypothesis: Emotional Stability judgments focus on eyes
   - Hypothesis: Agreeableness judgments focus on overall face expression
   - Compare AOI dwell times across traits (trait x AOI ANOVA)

4. TRAIT CONFUSION ANALYSIS
   - When participants are wrong, is there a pattern?
   - Do certain high/low video pairs cause more confusion?
   - Are errors random or systematic (e.g., always choosing more attractive face)?

5. EFFECT SIZE COMPARISON
   - Cohen's d for each trait (accuracy vs chance)
   - Which traits show strongest behavioral signal?
   - Forest plot of effect sizes across traits

6. INTER-TRAIT CORRELATIONS
   - Per participant: does being good at one trait predict being good at another?
   - Correlation matrix of per-participant accuracy across traits
   - Are there "super-perceivers" who are good at all traits?

STATISTICAL METHODS:
- One-way repeated-measures ANOVA (5 traits)
- Post-hoc pairwise t-tests with correction
- Two-way ANOVA (trait x AOI)
- Pearson correlations (inter-trait accuracy)
- Effect size: Cohen's d, partial eta-squared

OUTPUT FILES TO GENERATE:
- trait_ranking.csv
- trait_accuracy_comparison.png
- trait_difficulty_radar.png
- trait_gaze_patterns.png
- inter_trait_correlation_matrix.png
