05 - GAZE PATTERN ANALYSIS
===========================

PURPOSE: Deep dive into WHERE people look on faces when forming personality
judgments. This is the core eye-tracking contribution of your study.

DATA NEEDED:
- Eye tracking fixation data (x, y coordinates, duration)
- Video frame dimensions and face landmark positions
- AOI definitions for face regions

PREREQUISITE - FACE AOI DEFINITION:
- Use face landmark detection (e.g., dlib, MediaPipe) on stimulus videos
- Define AOIs: left eye, right eye, nose, mouth, forehead, chin, left cheek, right cheek
- Map gaze coordinates to AOIs per frame (since videos show moving faces)

ANALYSES TO RUN:
----------------

1. GAZE HEATMAPS
   - Aggregate gaze data across all participants for each video
   - Create heatmaps overlaid on representative video frames
   - Compare heatmaps: correct vs incorrect trials
   - Compare heatmaps: high confidence vs low confidence trials
   - Visualization: heatmap grid (trait x accuracy)

2. FACE REGION DWELL TIME
   - Proportion of viewing time on each face AOI
   - Eyes vs mouth dominance: which region gets more attention?
   - Dwell time ratios across traits
   - Literature comparison: face processing studies show eye-region dominance

3. FIRST FIXATION ANALYSIS
   - Where do people look first on each face video?
   - First fixation location distribution (eyes? nose? center?)
   - First fixation duration: longer = more initial processing
   - Does first fixation location predict response accuracy?

4. GAZE TRANSITIONS (CRITICAL FOR YOUR STUDY)
   - Number of transitions between Video 1 and Video 2 regions
   - "Comparison behavior": how often do participants mentally compare?
   - NOTE: In your sequential design, this means comparing fixation patterns
     during Video 1 vs Video 2, not simultaneous transitions
   - Within each video: transitions between face AOIs

5. CHOSEN vs UNCHOSEN VIDEO GAZE
   - Total dwell time on the video eventually chosen vs not chosen
   - "Gaze cascade effect" (Shimojo et al., 2003):
     People tend to look more at the option they will choose
   - This is a strong finding to replicate in personality perception context

6. GAZE CONSISTENCY ACROSS PARTICIPANTS
   - Do different people look at the same face regions?
   - Inter-subject correlation of gaze patterns
   - Normalized scanpath similarity (using string-edit distance or correlation)
   - High consistency = face region is objectively important for that trait

7. GAZE ENTROPY
   - Shannon entropy of gaze distribution across AOIs
   - Higher entropy = more distributed/exploratory looking
   - Lower entropy = more focused looking
   - Compare entropy across traits and correct/incorrect trials
   - Hypothesis: more difficult traits produce higher gaze entropy

STATISTICAL METHODS:
- Repeated-measures ANOVA (AOI x trait on dwell time)
- Chi-square (first fixation location distribution)
- Paired t-test (chosen vs unchosen dwell time)
- Mixed-effects models (participant random, trait/AOI fixed)
- Permutation tests for scanpath similarity

OUTPUT FILES TO GENERATE:
- heatmaps/ (per trait, per video)
- aoi_dwell_proportions.csv
- first_fixation_analysis.csv
- gaze_transitions.csv
- gaze_entropy_by_trait.png
- chosen_vs_unchosen_dwell.png
