07 - INDIVIDUAL DIFFERENCES ANALYSIS
=====================================

PURPOSE: Examine participant-level variation. Are some people better at
perceiving personality? Do different people use different visual strategies?

DATA NEEDED:
- All behavioral data aggregated per participant
- Eye tracking data aggregated per participant

ANALYSES TO RUN:
----------------

1. PARTICIPANT ACCURACY DISTRIBUTION
   - Distribution of per-participant mean accuracy (across all 125 trials)
   - Range: best vs worst performer
   - Are there "super-perceivers" (significantly above chance on all traits)?
   - Are there participants performing at chance (potential non-engagers)?

2. PARTICIPANT GAZE STRATEGY CLUSTERS
   - Cluster participants based on their gaze patterns:
     * Eye-focused viewers (spend most time on eyes)
     * Mouth-focused viewers
     * Holistic viewers (distributed across face)
   - K-means or hierarchical clustering on AOI dwell proportions
   - Do different gaze strategies predict different accuracy levels?

3. CONSISTENCY ACROSS TRAITS
   - Per participant: accuracy profile across 5 traits
   - Are some people good at ALL traits or specialized?
   - ICC (intraclass correlation) for within-person consistency
   - Profile analysis: cluster participants by their trait accuracy pattern

4. RESPONSE BIAS
   - Does any participant always choose Video 1 or Video 2?
   - Position bias: proportion of "first" vs "second" responses
   - Extreme bias (>70% one option) may indicate non-engagement
   - Flag these participants for potential exclusion

5. OUTLIER DETECTION
   - Identify participants with:
     * Very fast RT (<500ms mean, suggesting random clicking)
     * Very slow RT (>10s mean, suggesting distraction)
     * Near-chance accuracy across all traits
     * Poor eye tracking data quality (>30% data loss)
   - Decision: exclude or analyze separately

STATISTICAL METHODS:
- Descriptive statistics per participant
- K-means / hierarchical clustering
- ICC (intraclass correlation coefficient)
- Binomial test (response bias detection)
- Mixed-effects models with participant as random intercept

OUTPUT FILES TO GENERATE:
- participant_summary.csv
- accuracy_distribution.png
- gaze_strategy_clusters.png
- participant_trait_profiles.png
- exclusion_criteria_report.txt
